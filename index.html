DayMate – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

DayMate is an everyday personal organizer designed to help users manage tasks, reminders, notes, lists, and important dates. We value your privacy and aim to keep your personal information under your control.

2. Information Collection

DayMate is designed to operate without requiring users to create an account. Its core functionality stores user-created content locally on the device.

3. Data Storage

Tasks, reminders, notes, lists, and important dates are stored locally on your device. DayMate does not require cloud synchronization for its core functionality.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. User-created content is not transmitted to our servers as part of the local-only functionality. Manually exported backup files are under the user's control.

5. Notifications

If notification functionality is enabled, DayMate may schedule local notifications on the device.

8. Data Deletion

Users can remove information by deleting individual entries or clearing the app's stored data. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored information. Export a backup before uninstalling if you want to preserve your data.
