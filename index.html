# DayMate – Privacy Policy **Last updated:** November 8, 2026 ## 1. Introduction DayMate is an everyday personal organizer designed to help users manage tasks, reminders, notes, lists, and important dates. We value your privacy and aim to keep your personal information under your control. ## 2. Information Collection DayMate is designed to operate without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information. The app's core functionality stores user-created content locally on the device. ## 3. Data Storage Tasks, reminders, notes, lists, and important dates are stored locally on your device. DayMate does not require cloud synchronization or an online account for its core functionality. ## 4. Data Sharing We do not sell users' personal information. User-created content is not transmitted to our servers as part of the local-only functionality. If users manually export a backup, the resulting file is under their control and may be shared by them independently. ## 5. Notifications If notification functionality is enabled, DayMate may schedule local notifications on the device to remind users about their activities. ## 6. Data Deletion Users can remove their information by deleting individual entries or clearing the app's stored data. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored information. Users should export a backup before uninstalling if they want to preserve their data. ## 7. Children's Privacy DayMate does not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or privacy practices change. ## 9.
